1 - About this project

We are both part of a community of women designers and wanted to create something meaningful for women — not grand, but small enough to bring to life in 2 months.

As we explored different directions, we created this space — a collection of questions and reflections designed to spark curiosity rather than provide definitive answers.

The idea took shape after discovering Rosalia's LUX Album — a collection of thoughts gathered around a single theme. It inspired us to explore a similar format, centered on the questions many women quietly carry throughout their lives.

The visual language was inspired by The Cut's editorial flow of the Behavioral Architecture website, creating space for the words to take the lead.

Copy was written with assistance of ChatGPT and then edited.
